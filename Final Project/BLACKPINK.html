Korean Music History

  • Blackpink released their debut single album, Square One, on August 8, 2016, consisting of tracks "Boombayah" and "Whistle".

  • Blackpink released their second single album, Square Two, consisting of tracks "Playing with Fire" and "Stay", on November 1, 2016.

  • On June 22, 2017, the group released their first standalone digital single, "As If It's Your Last".

  • On June 15, 2018, the group released their first Korean-language EP, Square Up, consisting of 4 tracks "DDU-DU DDU-DU", "Forever Young", "Really" and "See U Later".

  • On October 19, 2018, English singer Dua Lipa released "Kiss and Make Up" with Blackpink, a new track on the re-released edition of her self-titled debut album.

  • KILL THIS LOVE, was released on April 5, 2019, alongside its lead single of the same name "Kill This Love", along with 3 other tracks "Don't Know What To Do", "Kick It" and "Hope Not".

  • Lady Gaga confirmed that Blackpink would be featured on the track list of her sixth studio album, Chromatica. Their collaboration, "Sour Candy", was released as a promotional single on May 28, 2020.

  • The single "How You Like That" was heavily teased on social media in the lead-up to its digital release on June 26, 2020, Followed by YG Entertainment announcing that a second single, "Ice Cream" with American singer Selena Gomez, would be released on August 28, 2020, Then Finally Blackpink released their first Korean studio album, THE ALBUM, on October 2, 2020, with "Lovesick Girls" as its third and main single, along with 5 other new tracks "Pretty Savage", "Bet You Wanna" featuring Cardi B, "Crazy Over You", "Love To Hate Me" and "You Never Know".

  • It was announced that Blackpink would release a pre-release single titled "Pink Venom" on August 19, 2022, And then On September 16, 2022, Blackpink released their second studio album, BORN PINK, with 8 total songs, one being the single "Shut Down", "Typa Girl", "Yeah Yeah Yeah", "Hard To Love" a surprise solo for the member Rosé, "The Happiest Girl', "Tally" and "Ready For Love" which was previously only released on July 29, 2022 with an animated music video YouTube in collaboration with the video game PUBG Mobile.

  • The group announced a song titled "THE GIRLS", which was released as part of the soundtrack for their mobile game, Blackpink: The Game, on August 25, 2023.

  • Following its debut performance on July 5, the single "Jump" was officially released on July 11, 2025, nearly 3 years after their last offical comeback in 2022.

  • On January 14, 2026, Blackpink officially announced their third Korean extended play Deadline, due for release on February 27.
